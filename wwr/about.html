History

Founded in 1914 in a small farm, High Side began with a simple goal: get somewhere great to raft. Founder Heines Von Müller started with a single raft and a small plot of land. By 1918, demand grew beyond Kirn, prompting the company to expand.

In 1939, High Side opened dozens of routes and cemented its place among the great rafting companies. Today, the company receives over 2000 customers from first-time thrill-seekers and weekend campers to experienced paddlers and certified river guides.
